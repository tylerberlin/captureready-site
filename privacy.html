Privacy Policy

Last updated July 8, 2026

CaptureReady is developed by Tyler Berlin. This policy explains what information CaptureReady uses, where it lives, and who — if anyone — it's shared with. In short: your data stays on your device. We don't run a backend server, we don't have user accounts, and we don't collect or sell your information.

Information CaptureReady Uses

CaptureReady uses the following on-device data to power its features. None of it is transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

DataWhy it's usedWhere it's stored
LocationLocal weather, sun/moon times, golden & blue hour, and tagging shoot locations on the mapOn your device only
Photos you choose to attachJournal entries and location pins you createOn your device only
CameraCapturing reference photos for your shoot journal, if you choose toOn your device only
Gear, presets, shoots, journal entriesCore app functionality — saved settings, equipment, and notesOn your device only, via Apple's SwiftData framework

You can deny location, camera, or photo library access at any time in iOS Settings → CaptureReady. Some features (like local weather or map tagging) won't work without location access, but the rest of the app functions normally.

Third-Party Services

CaptureReady uses the following third-party services strictly to fetch data the app displays to you:

  • Apple WeatherKit — provides live weather conditions for your location. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo — a free, no-API-key weather service CaptureReady falls back to if WeatherKit is temporarily unavailable. See Open-Meteo's terms.
  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — powers location search and the Photography Map. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy above.

These requests include only the coordinates or search text needed to return a result (e.g., "what's the weather at this latitude/longitude"). No account, advertising, or analytics identifier is attached to these requests by CaptureReady.

What We Don't Do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile creation
  • No analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or advertising networks
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your data with third parties for marketing
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind

Data Retention & Deletion

Because everything is stored locally in the app's on-device database, deleting the app deletes your CaptureReady data along with it. If you back up your device via iCloud or iTunes/Finder, your CaptureReady data may be included in that backup, subject to Apple's backup encryption and your own iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

CaptureReady is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If CaptureReady's data practices change — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud sync or account feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
