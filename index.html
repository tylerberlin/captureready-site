Support

Questions? We're here to help.

Why does my weather data say "Estimated"?

CaptureReady uses Apple WeatherKit for live weather. If WeatherKit is briefly unavailable (no connection, or a temporary outage), CaptureReady automatically falls back to a free alternate weather source, and only shows placeholder estimates as a last resort when neither is reachable. Pull down to refresh once you're back online.

Why does it take a moment to find my location?

A first location fix can take a few seconds to around 15–20 seconds, especially indoors where your device has to fall back from GPS to Wi‑Fi based positioning. This is normal iOS behavior. If it can't get a fix within 15 seconds, CaptureReady lets you tap to retry, or search for a location manually instead.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you save in CaptureReady — shoots, gear, presets, journal entries, and pinned locations — is stored privately on your device. It isn't uploaded to any server we operate. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why does the Exposure Calculator limit my ISO or aperture range?

If you've marked a camera or lens as "active" in Gear, CaptureReady limits the Exposure Triangle and Calculator to what that specific gear actually supports. Turn off the active toggle on that item (or don't mark any gear active) to use the full standard range instead.
