Support

Questions? We're here to help.

Why does my weather data say "Estimated"?

CaptureReady uses Apple Weather for live conditions. If it's briefly unavailable (no connection, or a temporary outage), CaptureReady automatically falls back to a free alternate weather source, and only shows placeholder estimates as a last resort when neither is reachable. Pull down to refresh once you're back online.

Why does it take a moment to find my location?

A first location fix can take a few seconds to around 15–20 seconds, especially indoors where your device has to fall back from GPS to Wi‑Fi based positioning. This is normal iOS behavior. If it can't get a fix within 15 seconds, CaptureReady lets you tap to retry, or search for a location manually instead.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you save — shoots, gear, presets, journal entries, and pinned locations — lives on your device and syncs to your own private iCloud, so a lost phone doesn't mean a lost library. It never touches a server we operate, and we have no way to see it. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, it simply stays local and the app tells you so under More → Settings → Backup. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why don't I see the AI Camera Assistant?

The assistant runs on Apple Intelligence, which needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence switched on in iOS Settings. On devices that can't run it the tab is hidden rather than shown broken. Everything else in the app works exactly the same, and the exposure maths behind the assistant is calculated either way — only the written explanation needs the model.

My camera isn't in the gear database. Now what?

The database covers over 3,600 bodies, but not every one ever made. Add the camera by hand in Gear and type its ISO and shutter ranges from the manual; everything downstream works identically. Lenses never need the database at all — type the full lens name and CaptureReady reads the focal length, maximum aperture, and stabilisation straight out of it.

Why does the Exposure Calculator limit my ISO or aperture range?

If you've marked a camera or lens as "active" in Gear, CaptureReady limits the Exposure Triangle and Calculator to what that specific gear actually supports. Turn off the active toggle on that item (or don't mark any gear active) to use the full standard range instead.
